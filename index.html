CO-STAR Framework (Comprehensive Context)

Ideal for complex deliverables, strategic consulting, and long-form technical syntheses.

  • CContext: Provide essential background circumstances.
  • OObjective: Explicitly state the exact task to accomplish.
  • SStyle: Dictate writing cadence (e.g. academic, journalistic).
  • TTone: Establish emotional stance (e.g. authoritative, friendly).
  • AAudience: Calibrate technical depth to the intended reader.
  • RResponse: Enforce strict output format (JSON, Markdown, code).
RTF Framework (Rapid Execution)

Perfect for rapid coding tasks, refactoring, quick quizzes, and concise copy generation.

  • RRole: "Act as a Principal Security Architect..."
  • TTask: "Audit this endpoint for OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities..."
  • FFormat: "Return results formatted as a Markdown table with severity ratings..."
💡 Pro-Tip: Always include negative constraints ("Do not include pleasantries, do not assume third-party APIs") to prevent conversational fluff.
